# Ballerz — Decision Log & Next Design Sprint

**20 November 2026.** Draft, not yet approved.

## Confirmed vocabulary and intent

- **Ballerz** is the football-management game.
- **Baller Bucks** is the proposed game currency/token. Names may change.
- The founder prefers an accessible game (roughly £100/year baseline cost floated), with utility over speculation.
- AI assistant managers are intended to be real task-performing agents and a possible subscription product.
- Players, clubs, stadiums, leagues and federation-like entities may become NFTs.
- Sponsorships, bursaries and stadium gate receipts are ideas for an economically connected football world.

## Proposed approach to validate next

1. **Choose the Baller Bucks role.** Separate stable game accounting from a speculative/tradeable token discussion. Resolve the historical GBP-pegged BBSC design against current interest in demand-driven appreciation.
2. **Define one club-season financial ledger.** Draw inflows and outflows in game units, then identify exactly who funds every Baller Bucks transfer. Test a small budget and an expensive stadium.
3. **Define 3 NFT rights bundles.** Club, Stadium, League: each needs owner privileges, metadata, transfer constraints and enforceable rules.
4. **Prototype one AI assistant workflow.** Example: scout three under-21 midfielders, present evidence and costs, then propose rather than auto-buy.
5. **Validate economics.** Stress-test free players, fee indexing, league rewards, sponsor grants, AI inference costs, token price shocks and wealth concentration.
6. **Map the current implementation.** The founder confirmed `DarthShoge/Ballerz-NFT` as the canonical repository; reconcile older smart contracts with the proposed Solana architecture.
