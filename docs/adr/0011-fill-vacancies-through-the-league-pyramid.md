# Fill vacancies through the league pyramid

Keep the final tables and apply scheduled promotion and relegation before filling vacancies. A departed team in a relegation place counts toward the division's scheduled exits. If a departed team finishes in a promotion place, pass that promotion to the next eligible finisher.

Fill remaining vacancies from the top division down. Offer each place to the highest eligible finisher who competed in the division immediately below and was due to stay there. Preserve the scheduled moves and limit each team to one division change. Admit new teams at the bottom. If the league cannot fill an eligible bottom division, reorganise it before the next funded season.

Keep a bottom division below its target size if it still meets the eight-team funding minimum. If it falls below eight, apply the published sporting moves first, then use the fewest structural moves needed to keep both bottom divisions eligible. Move the lowest-placed eligible teams that were due to stay in the tier immediately above. Merge the bottom two when there are too few teams to fund them separately and the combined division fits the league's structure.

After a merge, retain the previous tables and the record of the published sporting moves. Moves across surviving boundaries take effect. Publish the new movement counts before kickoff for the boundaries that still exist, keeping promotion and relegation places distinct.

Publish the intended division sizes, standings rule and registration deadline before the season that will decide places in a restored bottom division. At rollover, preserve scheduled moves across existing boundaries. Place new entrants in the restored bottom division and use the latest standings to assign the lowest-placed eligible incumbents due to stay in the merged tier wherever the new capacities require it. Activate the split when enough eligible entrants have committed and both divisions can be funded. If recruitment falls short, retain the merged division.

Offer places vacated before kickoff to eligible reserves with a published acceptance deadline. Recheck the eight-team funding minimum before the first fixture. If both divisions still qualify, use the revised sizes. If either falls below eight, rebalance when possible or retain a single division. Publish the final lists, fixtures, BB rates and movement rules before kickoff. Once play starts, use the approved schedule and resolve later departures as forfeits.

Order reserves by the recruitment window in which they submitted a complete application. Use one published draw to rank teams within the same window. Publish the reserve order before vacancies open, and confirm eligibility before making each offer. If a reserve declines, lets the offer expire or is ineligible, offer the place to the next eligible reserve in order.

A team that declines or lets a valid offer expire may opt back in during the next recruitment window, behind teams still waiting for their first offer. A valid offer matches the published terms and gives the team the full acceptance window. A team keeps its original priority if it never received a valid offer or if the league withdrew the offer.

If a team accepts a valid reserve offer and then voluntarily withdraws before kickoff, offer its place to the next eligible reserve. The team can opt back in during the next recruitment window behind teams still waiting for a first offer. If the league cancels the place or materially changes the terms after acceptance, the team retains its original priority.
