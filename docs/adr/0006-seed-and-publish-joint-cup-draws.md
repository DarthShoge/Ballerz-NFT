# Seed and publish joint cup draws

The first joint cup has eight places, four per league. Each league's two highest-finishing confirmed entrants from its last completed season are seeded and placed in opposite halves. Draw seeds against unseeded entrants, choosing randomly among pairings that maximise interleague quarter-finals.

A league fills declined places from its eligible reserves. Any remaining vacancy stays with that league's quota and gives a bye to a top seed from the other league. The draw requires at least six confirmed entrants and at least two from each league. If entries fall short, the organiser may extend the deadline with both leagues' approval or cancel the edition before the draw.

Publish the confirmed entrants, seed pots, byes and pairings together. Publication makes the bracket official. A sale carries the occupied slot with the Team NFT, and any later withdrawal is handled within the published bracket.
