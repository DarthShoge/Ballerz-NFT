# Represent competitions as transferable NFTs

A League NFT or Cup NFT gives its holder the right to appoint competition admins. The rules, fixtures, standings and existing obligations stay with the competition when ownership changes.

One owner holds the Cup NFT and appoints the lead organiser. In a joint cup, all participating league admins approve the entry quotas, format and dates before entries open. Each league nominates teams, and each team owner accepts their place before the draw. A declined place passes to the next eligible team from the same league.

The lead organiser reserves dates with the participating leagues before opening entries. The first joint cup uses a single-leg knockout bracket.
