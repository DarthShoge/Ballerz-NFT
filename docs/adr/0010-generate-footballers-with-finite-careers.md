# Generate footballers with finite careers

Ballerz generates original tradeable footballers in controlled, scheduled intakes. The generator targets the active professional population across playing levels and calibrates rarity by position and skill profile. Initial rarity targets are estimates until supported by representative data.

Current ability and potential are separate. Footballers develop, decline and retire. Retirement removes them from the playable supply and preserves their career history on the Footballer NFT.

The game engine determines when a footballer retires. A qualifying retirement earns the team a one-time credit toward a signing from a scheduled youth intake, beginning with the next intake. The new footballer has their own ability and potential and comes from the planned supply for that intake.

At the shared year-end, the engine rolls once for each eligible footballer using their age and position and stores the outcome. Ownership changes leave the result in place. The first outfield test curve uses annual probabilities of 5%, 10%, 20%, 35%, 55%, 75% and 100% at ages 32 through 38. Goalkeepers use a separate, later curve.

The first eligibility test requires five in-game years of service with the same Team NFT and 60 competitive appearances spread across at least three in-game years. The team that holds the footballer at retirement can claim based on service with that team. Selling the footballer ends the selling team's eligibility and starts the buyer's service from zero. Selling the Team NFT preserves service.

A qualifying retiree who spent at least two years as an elite footballer with that team earns a succession claim. Reserve eligible high-potential prospects within the planned intake for those claims.

Show a prospect's position, current ability, scouted potential and fee after credit. Teams rank the prospects they would sign at those fees. Allocate the highest-ranked available choice when each team's turn comes. Each team gets one pick per pass, ordered by the intake in which its oldest funded claim still in the allocation was earned. A published random draw breaks ties. Unfilled claims carry forward with their original priority.

Keep some wonderkids available on the regular market. The first test reserves five of ten for succession claims. Any unused reserved prospects go to the regular market after allocation.

An outstanding succession claim follows the Team NFT through a sale and retains its earned intake and queue priority. Show outstanding claims and their earned dates in the team listing. The buyer can rank prospects for inherited claims and pays the fee after credit. The claim has no separate transfer, and the retired Footballer NFT carries the footballer's career history.

If the sale is confirmed before the ranking deadline, retain the previous owner's ranking as a draft. The buyer may approve, reorder or replace it by the original deadline. If the sale is confirmed after the deadline and before allocation, the buyer can approve the exact locked ranking and the disclosed fees after credit as part of the sale. Approval commits the buyer to sign the highest-ranked available prospect when their turn comes. Without the buyer's approval, the claim carries forward with its original priority.

Approving a shortlist reserves the highest disclosed fee after credit for that claim. Approvals for multiple claims require separate reservations. Settle the actual fee, add the prospect to the squad and redeem the claim together. Release any excess BB at settlement, or the full reservation if no prospect is allocated. An unfilled claim keeps its original priority.

On a Team NFT sale, release the seller's reservation at handover. The buyer must fund any shortlist they adopt with unreserved BB available after paying for the team. Before the deadline they can adjust the list to fit their budget. After the deadline they can fund the exact locked list or carry the claim forward.

Teams choose which claims to fund by the ranking deadline. Each funded claim uses its own earned intake for priority. Claims the team leaves unfunded keep their own priorities for later intakes. Releasing BB during allocation does not reopen the intake for claims the team left unfunded.

After a total of three eligible intakes in which a succession claim goes unfunded, it loses reserved access and queue priority. A funded claim that receives no prospect, or an intake with no reserved prospects available to the team, does not count as a skip. The skip count follows the claim when the Team NFT is sold. After expiry, the team keeps the one-time retirement credit for a regular youth signing.

A retirement credit for a regular youth signing has no expiry date. It stays with the Team NFT until the team redeems it on an eligible signing.

Each youth signing can use one retirement credit. The team keeps any other credits for future signings.
