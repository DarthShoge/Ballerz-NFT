# Schedule league fixtures

League fixtures have a published kickoff, so both managers can submit early without advancing the schedule. A team must submit a valid team sheet by the fixture deadline to avoid a forfeit and can return for the next fixture. Manager-arranged friendlies can run when both managers are ready.

If one team forfeits before kickoff, the other receives a 3–0 administrative win and three points. If both forfeit, each records one match played, one loss and zero points. A double forfeit has no score or effect on goal difference. Forfeits before kickoff do not award footballer goals or appearances.

Abandon a match if either team has fewer than seven players after legal substitutions. If only one team falls below seven, award three points and a 3–0 administrative win to the opponent. Preserve the score at stoppage as the official result if the opponent already led by at least three goals. Keep the original stoppage score in the match report.

Resolve all injuries, dismissals and legal substitutions from the same stoppage before checking either team's player count. If both are below seven, record one match played, one loss and zero points for each team. There is no official score or effect on goal difference. Keep the score at abandonment in the match report.

Footballers keep goals, assists, appearances and minutes from the time actually played before abandonment. Administrative scores are used for the league table and do not award individual goals or assists. Red cards from play still carry their normal suspensions, including when both teams fall below seven.

A suspension follows the Footballer NFT across teams and leagues. Show the remaining matches in a transfer listing. In v1, serve one match of the suspension per completed official Ballerz league or cup fixture for the footballer's registered team. Friendlies, pre-kickoff forfeits and abandoned matches do not count.

If the footballer is sold after the seller's team-sheet deadline, keep the suspension tied to that locked fixture until it is resolved. A completed match serves one game of the suspension. A pre-kickoff forfeit or abandonment leaves the remaining suspension intact. The buyer can start serving it at their first eligible team-sheet deadline after the seller's fixture is resolved.

If an official postponement cancels the seller's team-sheet lock, release the footballer from that fixture without serving a match of the suspension. The buyer's first eligible fixture with a deadline after the release can count if completed. A kickoff delay that retains the team-sheet lock keeps the suspension tied to the original fixture.

On an ordinary loan, the parent team retains the Footballer NFT and the borrowing team holds match registration. The suspension stays with the footballer, and eligible completed fixtures for the borrowing team count toward serving it. The first playtest uses a four-in-game-week minimum loan term before the player can register again with the parent team.

Apply the team-sheet cutoff to loan starts and returns. If the loan ends after the borrowing team's team-sheet deadline, its locked fixture remains attached to the suspension and counts if completed. A pre-kickoff forfeit, abandonment or postponement that cancels the lock releases it without a deduction. The parent's fixtures can count from the first eligible deadline after the loan ends and the lock is released.

If the borrower named an eligible loanee in its locked starting eleven before the loan ended, the player can still play that fixture for the borrower. The parent can select the player from its first eligible team-sheet deadline after the fixture is resolved. An official postponement that cancels the original team-sheet lock also ends the borrower's permission to field the player.

A Footballer NFT sale during a loan transfers the parent team's role and existing loan terms to the buyer. The borrowing team keeps match registration until the agreed end date. Its eligible completed fixtures count toward any suspension, including a fixture that locked before the loan ended. Disclose the loan end date, wage split and remaining suspension in the sale listing.

A loan normally continues when the borrower goes into wage arrears. The parent may recall the footballer early if the loan contains an agreed arrears recall clause, the four-in-game-week minimum has passed, and the borrower is still in arrears. Apply the usual team-sheet cutoff to the recall. The borrower remains responsible for its accrued share of wages. The parent accrues the full wage from the confirmed recall.

Recheck the borrower's arrears when confirming a pending recall. Full repayment before confirmation cancels the request and the loan continues. A partial payment leaves the recall eligible. Repayment after confirmation clears the debt and the confirmed recall stands, even if a locked fixture still ties the player to the borrower.

At each settlement cutoff, apply confirmed BB payments before confirming recalls. If the payments clear the arrears, cancel the recall. A payment still pending at the cutoff does not count, even if it was submitted earlier. A later confirmation can clear the debt but does not undo a recall already confirmed.

Publish the Solana confirmation level required for BB payments and record a chain checkpoint at each settlement cutoff. Keep recalls pending until Ballerz has reconciled the payments through that checkpoint. A payment confirmed by the cutoff counts even if Ballerz observes it later. Correct a recall that was marked confirmed based on stale data if a timely payment cleared the arrears.

If an outage prevents recording the checkpoint, keep the recall pending and announce the next settlement cutoff at which Ballerz can record a verified checkpoint. Apply all eligible confirmed payments through the replacement cutoff before deciding the recall. If the original checkpoint exists, use it to reconcile payments even if their notifications arrived later.

If the loan reaches its agreed end while a recall is pending, end the loan as scheduled and close the recall request. Use the agreed end date for the wage handover. The borrower's accrued share and arrears remain its responsibility. Apply the ordinary loan-return rules to any fixture that locked before the loan ended.
