# Resolve unplayed knockout ties

Before the final, a double forfeit eliminates both teams and leaves the associated slot in the next round empty. A team paired with an empty slot advances by bye before the final. Two empty slots produce an empty slot in the next round.

After the draw, a team that withdraws forfeits its next tie and leaves the edition. The existing bracket and completed results stand. Its opponent wins by walkover if it submits a valid team sheet by the deadline, and otherwise the tie is a double forfeit. A team sale does not undo a withdrawal.

The final requires a valid team sheet by the deadline even when one slot is empty. One ready finalist wins by walkover, without a simulated score or footballer statistics. With zero ready finalists, the edition closes without a champion. If both finalists miss the deadline, the final is recorded as a double forfeit and both teams' paths to it are kept in the edition's history.

When a verified Ballerz outage prevents submissions, the lead organiser moves the fixture to a reserve date, publishes the reason and reopens submissions for both teams under the same deadline.
