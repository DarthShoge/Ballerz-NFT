# Fund first squads with BallerBuckz

Use one game-only currency, BallerBuckz (BB), across all leagues in v1. Each qualifying new team receives the same starter bursary and can buy from a shared starter pool with enough affordable footballers to fill a starting eleven and bench. An affordable one-click squad offers a quick route into play.

Rarity informs player supply and opening prices. Teams set their own BB prices for secondary transfers, with indicative ranges shown by Ballerz.

An owner wallet qualifies for one starter bursary when its team accepts its first league place. Bursary spending covers the starter pool and approved startup costs. Treasury receipts from the starter pool fund future bursaries, with a seasonal limit on fresh BB issuance.

If a team withdraws before kickoff, it keeps its signings and the remaining bursary freezes until the team accepts another funded place. The original grant counts as the wallet's one bursary.

The team earns the bursary subsidy on its signings by staying through the end of a funded season and being ready for at least 85% of fixtures. Until then, selling a bursary-funded footballer requires full repayment of the portion the bursary covered. A Team NFT sale requires repayment of the remaining unearned subsidy on its funded signings. Sale proceeds can fund repayment. If the proceeds fall short, the seller covers the difference with unrestricted BB before the transfer settles.

A team can voluntarily return a footballer with an unearned bursary subsidy to the starter pool. The team gives up the Footballer NFT and the treasury waives the subsidy repayment for that footballer. The return provides no BB payout or restored bursary credit, and BB the team contributed to the signing remains spent. Wages stop accruing when the return is confirmed. Wages already accrued remain due at year-end.

The first balance test uses a 5 million BB bursary for an 18-player squad. The reference squad costs 3.8 million BB. Replacing one 250,000 BB starter with a 1 million BB prospect brings the squad to 4.55 million BB. These amounts are test inputs for affordability and season costs.

Use 4 million BB as a test opening price for an established elite footballer. Owners set their own resale prices. The first competitive playtest uses the bursary and BB earned through play or transfers. Paid top-ups are a separate decision.

Set the league reward budget before the season. Teams receive a base BB payment for playing league fixtures and smaller bonuses for results. Footballers have recurring BB wages in the first playtest. Show the wage alongside the transfer fee so teams can budget for the signing and its ongoing cost.

Wages go to the Ballerz treasury and can be recycled through the published season budget. Track BB held by teams, treasury receipts and fresh issuance separately.

The match reward test divides a 2,280,000 BB annual base allowance by the greater of 18 or the team's approved fixture count. Publish and lock the base rate before kickoff. Each Team NFT has one allowance per wage year, even if it plays more than one short season or changes divisions. A team with fewer than 18 approved fixtures can earn only the corresponding fraction of the allowance. The 38-fixture reference schedule pays a 60,000 BB base per match.

Ballerz approves funded entrants and schedules before committing treasury funds. A funded division requires at least eight admitted teams with distinct owner wallets. A wallet can fill one funded seat across the league's divisions. At most four meetings against the same opposing team in a wage year qualify for rewards.

Check the eight-team minimum when a division is funded and when it renews. If a funded division loses teams midseason, finish using the approved schedule and rates. Resolve the affected fixtures under the existing forfeit rules and retain unused reward funds in the treasury. Refill vacancies or reorganise the divisions before the next funded season.

Result bonuses apply to completed matches, with 10,000 BB for a win or 5,000 BB per team for a draw. In a one-sided pre-kickoff forfeit, pay the ready team the base amount and award the usual three league points. A double forfeit before kickoff pays neither team.

If a match is abandoned because one team falls below seven, the opponent keeps its base payment and completion credit if it qualified at kickoff. The short-handed team also keeps both if injuries alone made continuing impossible. If restoring the dismissed players would let it reach seven, classify its abandonment as disciplinary and withhold its BB and completion credit. Abandoned matches pay no result bonus.

If both teams fall below seven at the same stoppage, assess each team's BB and completion credit independently. A team keeps both if it qualified at kickoff and injuries alone made continuing impossible. Withhold both from a team whose abandonment was disciplinary.

Settle wages once at the shared year-end after match rewards. Show accrued wages throughout the year. Attribute wages in proportion to the time each team held the footballer, and apply the agreed wage split for any time on loan.

Set the wage split before a loan starts. The borrower pays 100% by default and the two teams can agree to a different split. Apply those percentages to the published wage rate for the time on loan. If the Footballer NFT changes hands during the loan, the seller keeps its accrued share and the buyer assumes the parent's share from the confirmed sale onward. Each team's unpaid share becomes its own wage arrears at year-end.

Set the footballer's rate by current ability and lock it until the next rollover. The rate follows the footballer through transfers. The annual test rates are 76,000 BB for rotation-level footballers, 152,000 BB for regular starters, 304,000 BB for top-level starters and 608,000 BB for established elite footballers. Designated wonderkids have a 304,000 BB minimum. The reference 18-player squad costs 2.204 million BB a year, or 2.356 million BB with the wonderkid in place of one regular starter.

When a team cannot cover its wage bill from available BB, pay what it can and record the shortfall as arrears. New income goes toward clearing arrears first. A team in arrears can fulfil its existing fixtures and sell footballers, but cannot spend on new signings or enter the next league season until the arrears clear.

The earlier emergency loan design allowed a team unable to field a legal starting eleven to borrow up to two rotation-level footballers. The pool retained ownership and charged the prorated 76,000 BB annual rotation wage at year-end. The loan ended when the team could field its own legal eleven or the season ended.

That design allowed Ballerz to mint emergency-only Footballer NFTs up to the verified shortage and loan limits. Each used the standard rotation-level profile and returned to the pool after the loan. These footballers were eligible only for emergency loans, with no secondary trades or retirement credits.

AI stand-ins are the default emergency cover in v1. Generate them as needed for the fixture with fixed minimum ability for the missing position, and label them on the team sheet. AI stand-ins cannot train or improve. The manager still has to submit a team sheet by the deadline. Entry into the next season requires the team's own legal eleven.

A team using AI cover can earn normal fixture BB and completion credit with up to two stand-ins per fixture for up to four fixtures per wage year. Outside either limit the team can play for points, but it earns no BB or completion credit for the fixture. Its opponent remains eligible under the normal rules.

Allow two additional fixtures with normal rewards and completion credit per wage year for verified injuries. The team must have at least 13 registered footballers, need at most two AI stand-ins, and be able to field a legal eleven if its injured footballers were fit. Apply the test to the available positions as well as the headcount. If injuries leave no available goalkeeper, a minimum-ability AI goalkeeper can qualify under the same test.

If the engine records a goalkeeper injury during play that prevents the goalkeeper continuing and there is no fit keeper on the bench, a minimum-ability AI goalkeeper can come on using a normal substitution. If the team has used all its substitutions, an outfielder takes over in goal. Use the kickoff eleven to determine AI cover allowances, BB eligibility and completion credit. A goalkeeper replacement during play uses neither cover allowance.

A goalkeeper red card reduces the number of players on the pitch by one. If there is no fit keeper on the bench and a substitution remains, bring on a minimum-ability AI goalkeeper in place of an outfielder. If substitutions are exhausted, move an outfielder into goal. In both cases the team plays a player down and the kickoff reward eligibility still applies.

The same forced replacement rules apply when an AI goalkeeper is injured or sent off. Use a fit goalkeeper from the bench if available. Otherwise another minimum-ability AI goalkeeper can enter through a remaining substitution. For an injury, take the injured goalkeeper's place. After a dismissal, take the place of an outfielder while preserving the reduction in team size.

The first completion-bonus test pays 150,000 BB to a team that stays through the end and is ready for at least 85% of its scheduled fixtures. A valid team sheet counts toward completion when the opponent forfeits. Each qualifying team receives another 20,500 BB for each team below it in the final table. The champion receives an additional 100,000 BB if it qualifies.

Use this reward curve across comparable divisions in the first competitive playtest. The maximum season-end payout for a 20-team division is 6,995,000 BB. With 380 fully played fixtures, the maximum total reward budget is 56,395,000 BB. Approve one treasury budget covering base payments, result bonuses and placement prizes across all divisions and short seasons in a wage year. If a division runs multiple short seasons, split its annual prize budget across them. Settle season-end prizes before annual wages. Measure team-sheet submission in the last five fixtures and re-entry the following season by finishing position.
