# Represent teams as transferable NFTs

A person mints a team as a transferable NFT. Selling the team transfers its squad as disclosed to the buyer, its competition place, its history, its remaining fixtures and its outstanding succession claims. Footballer NFTs may also be traded individually. If a team disbands midseason, completed results stand, its remaining league fixtures are forfeits, and its division place becomes vacant for the following season.

On confirmed sale, the previous owner's authority and manager appointment expire. The buyer can manage the team or appoint a manager. An existing valid team sheet remains in force and can be replaced up to the original deadline. After the deadline, the locked sheet stands.

Disclose wage arrears before a team sale. The seller can clear them at closing. Any remaining arrears transfer with the Team NFT and require the buyer's explicit acceptance.

A wallet may hold at most one team still in contention in the same cup edition. This limit applies to entry and to Team NFT transfers, including direct transfers and marketplace sales. The first version uses wallet ownership as the basis for the limit.

Accepting a cup place reserves the wallet's entry in that edition. Elimination, withdrawal or the end of the edition releases it. A nomination alone does not reserve a place.

A Team NFT transfer carries every live cup entry in the same Solana transaction. A conflict in any of those editions blocks the whole transfer. The buyer must approve the exact set of live cup entries, including for direct transfers and gifts. A change to that set requires fresh approval.

Withdrawal before the draw releases the sale restriction and allows the league to offer the place to a reserve. From publication of the draw until the edition closes, a wallet can hold at most one Team NFT from that draw. The restriction follows the Team NFT through intermediate sales and applies regardless of a team's results, forfeits or withdrawal. Leaving the bracket ends the live entry while the ownership restriction stays in force.
