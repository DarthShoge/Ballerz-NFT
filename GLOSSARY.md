# Ballerz

Ballerz is an online football management game built around competitions between teams and the ownership and trading of teams and footballers.

## Language

**Team**:
A persistent football team represented by a Team NFT. A team can change owners or be disbanded.
_Avoid_ Club.

**Team owner**:
The holder of a Team NFT.

**Manager**:
A person who selects a team's footballers and tactics for a match.

**Footballer**:
An individual represented by a Footballer NFT who can play in a Ballerz team. Regular footballers can be traded individually, and their NFTs preserve career history after retirement.
_Avoid_ Player when the meaning could be a person playing Ballerz.

**BallerBuckz (BB)**:
The shared in-game currency for earning, spending and trading across Ballerz leagues.

**Annual base allowance**:
The maximum in base league-fixture payments that a team can earn during one wage year.

**Wage arrears**:
The unpaid portion of a team's wage bill. Arrears are an obligation of the team.

**Starter bursary**:
An equal budget in BB that each qualifying new team receives to assemble its first squad.

**Starter pool**:
The shared pool of footballers offered to qualifying new teams.

**Intake**:
A scheduled group of newly issued Footballer NFTs entering the playable population.

**Current ability**:
A footballer's present playing ability.

**Potential**:
The level of playing ability a footballer could reach through development.

**Retirement credit**:
A team's one-time credit toward a signing from a scheduled youth intake after a footballer qualifies through service and retires.

**Wonderkid**:
A young footballer with elite potential.

**Succession claim**:
A team's right to access a wonderkid from a scheduled youth intake and apply its retirement credit, earned through the qualifying retirement of an established elite footballer. The claim transfers only with the Team NFT.

**League**:
A continuing competition represented by a League NFT, with one or more ranked divisions.

**Division**:
A tier of a league. Teams in the same division compete in the same standings.

**Promotion and relegation**:
Movement up or down between adjacent divisions at the end of a season according to the final standings and the league's published rules.

**League entry**:
A team's place in a league. A team enters the bottom division through automatic placement or an application approved by the league admin.

**Season**:
A period of league competition with its own fixtures, standings and champion. Teams, their squads and their history persist across seasons.

**In-game time**:
The compressed time over which the Ballerz football world advances.

**Wage year**:
The shared in-game period over which wages accrue. A division can play more than one season within a wage year.

**Competition owner**:
The holder of a League NFT or Cup NFT, with the right to appoint its admins.

**League admin**:
A person responsible for running a specific league and setting its competition rules.

**Cup**:
A competition represented by a Cup NFT. A cup can draw entrants from one or more leagues.

**Joint cup**:
A cup co-organised by multiple leagues. The participating league admins approve the rules before entries open and decide which teams to enter.

**Cup edition**:
One running of a cup, with its own rules, schedule and entrants.

**Live cup entry**:
A team's commitment to a cup edition, from acceptance of its place until elimination, withdrawal or the end of the edition.

**Seed**:
A confirmed cup entrant given a protected place in the draw based on its league finish.

**Bracket slot**:
A team's place in the published knockout bracket. An occupied slot belongs to the team and follows its Team NFT if ownership changes.

**Team withdrawal**:
A team's voluntary exit from a cup edition after accepting a place. A withdrawal after the draw forfeits the team's next tie.

**Cup ban**:
A sanction barring a team or a responsible wallet from the rest of the current edition and the next edition of the same cup. A team's ban stays with the Team NFT through a sale.

**Proxy control**:
An arrangement in which a registered owner or manager knowingly lets a banned person make the team's match decisions in the cup covered by the ban.

**Lead organiser**:
The person responsible for a joint cup's schedule and administration.

**Fixture**:
A pairing between two teams for a match.

**Match time**:
The elapsed time within a simulated match, expressed in football minutes.

**Friendly**:
A fixture arranged by managers outside official competition.

**League fixture**:
A scheduled fixture that forms part of a division's season.

**Team sheet**:
A team's starting eleven, formation and tactic for a fixture.

**Team-sheet deadline**:
The cutoff for submitting or revising a team sheet for a fixture.

**Emergency loan**:
Temporary cover for a team that cannot field a legal starting eleven during its season.

**AI stand-in**:
A simulated footballer who fills a missing lineup position. Its ability is fixed at the minimum standard for that position.

**Match report**:
The official result, match events, statistics and tactical notes for a fixture. It includes the score at the stoppage if a match is abandoned.

**Suspension**:
A disciplinary penalty that makes a footballer temporarily ineligible to play.

**Forfeit**:
An official fixture outcome when a team fails to fulfil its obligation to play. A team forfeits a league fixture if it misses the team-sheet deadline.

**Double forfeit**:
A fixture outcome in which both teams forfeit. In a league fixture, each team records a loss and earns zero points, with no score or change to goal difference.

**No contest**:
A league fixture closed without a competitive result because a verified disruption prevents fair play before year-end.

**Bye**:
Automatic advancement through a knockout round before the final when the other bracket slot is empty.

**Walkover**:
An official cup win awarded without playing a match. In the final, the sole team to submit a valid team sheet on time wins the title by walkover if the other team forfeits or its slot is empty.
